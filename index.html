00:01 — Introduction

From the camera
to the system.

A filmmaker who learned the frame the long way — now directing the systems that make content at scale.

Amit Das is a Mumbai-based AI Director and creative director whose career runs from the edit bay to the production control room to the generative model.

He began in post and agency video at The Glitch and Concept Productions, moved into artist and content management at OML, then spent five-plus years as an independent cinematographer and DOP — over a hundred branded films, OTT campaigns, episodic shows, music and fashion films, automotive and travel pieces, and social-first work for Netflix, Meta, Dell, Mahindra, Lakmé, Gillette, Agoda and many more.

He traveled to London on the marketing campaign for 83, embedded with Mumbai Indians through the IPL, directed two seasons of the NCPA Symphony Orchestra of India live from a six-camera PCR, and designed jewellery and product creatives for U.S. house Mercury Ring.

Today, as AI Director at T-Series, he builds end-to-end — generation, direction and edit — bringing that production foundation into AI-led creative direction with a luxury focus.

The real shift is in how he works: thinking in systems rather than single shots, and pairing deep production and marketing experience with generative pipelines to design, direct and deliver premium content at a scale that wasn’t possible before. The craft stays — the output multiplies. It’s the move from making one film at a time to leading the system that makes many.
